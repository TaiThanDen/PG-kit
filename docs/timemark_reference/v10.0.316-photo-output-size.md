# Matched photo output size

Target: LG-installed Timemark v10.0.316, base APK SHA-256
`b59c50b6c84ccfa180f8648bc6864a83040b61ff6fcd70eb7e092321042a1a98`.

REA `XCameraConfigService.getPictureSizeWithRatio(float)` delegates to the
selected aspect-ratio preset. Its four-argument overload prefers the stored
`widthxheight` preference, then a configured ResolutionItem, then the supplied
fallback. The 4:3 fallback is 2560 × 1920. Evidence is retained in
`reference_app/v10.0.316/pixel/rea-photo-size-policy.json`. The tested LG saves
1536 × 2048 or 2048 × 1536, establishing its current setting, not a universal
Timemark limit.

PG-kit now offers a 2048 px long-edge preset matching those observed samples,
and an original-camera-resolution preset. The native Android dialog is reached
from the existing menu button; this replaces a placeholder toast, and does not
claim to reproduce Timemark's entire settings screen. Selection persists in
the app's own preferences. The preset is frozen at shutter press along with
time, address and orientation.

Resizing occurs after EXIF normalization and landscape correction, before
drawing any watermark. It preserves aspect ratio and never enlarges smaller
images. JPEG quality follows the resulting dimensions through the existing
save path. The Activity and camera preview rotation rules are unchanged.

Verification on LG:

- Five size-policy unit tests passed, covering portrait/landscape 4:3, other
  ratios, no upscale, original size and invalid inputs. Existing tests passed.
- The resolution dialog was opened, both choices selected, reopened and
  dismissed with Back. The selected 2048 value was read from the app's own
  preferences. A 12-second screen recording and screenshots are ignored under
  `qa/output-resolution-ui/`; the recording was inspected as a frame sequence.
- Timemark and PG-kit saved real JPEGs at 15:39:13 and 15:39:28, both
  1536 × 2048 with EXIF orientation 1 and the same displayed 15:39 time,
  date, weekday and absent-address state. Ignored evidence is under
  `qa/matched-size-current-hold/`. No scaling or alignment is applied in
  `watermark-exact-pixels.png`.

The new capture removes the resolution/content confounders for the tested main
watermark. Camera exposure and scene still differ, and local verification-code
values differ by design. Foreground mask overlap remains below 1.0; this is not
proof of full pixel parity. The current hold was not reconfirmed by the user;
the previous four-direction physical verification remains the orientation
evidence. This change does not reproduce every remotely configured resolution.

## Xiaomi reference preset

On the Xiaomi running .316, fresh original JPEGs at 20:42 and 20:43 are
1920×2560 in `DCIM/Camera`, rather than the LG's selected 1536×2048. These
are actual pulled-file/MediaStore observations, consistent with the previously
traced configurable `getPictureSizeWithRatio` path. The app now offers a
separate 2560 px long-edge choice alongside 2048 px and camera original. No
preset is inferred from a device brand. The default remains 2048; tests cover
both orientations at 2560 and verify that smaller sources are never enlarged.
